# Minimal Journal Landing Story

## Summary

Reduced the Trainy landing page to the focused product story shown in the approved
reference screenshot. Deferred secondary product, role, and security sections until
the product narrative needs them.

## Page narrative

1. A spacious editorial hero focused on making each internship day worth remembering.
2. Three reasons journaling feels lightweight and useful.
3. A three-step daily rhythm: check in, capture the work, and reflect on growth.
4. A bold lime product belief statement.
5. A concise dark sign-in footer.

## Product behavior preserved

- LINE sign-in remains connected to the real authentication action.
- Thai and English copy remain available through the existing language toggle.
- The mobile menu, keyboard dismissal, semantic colors, and responsive layout remain
  intact.
- No runtime dependencies were added.

## Verification

- Inspected the live page in a mobile-width browser preview.
- Type checking, lint, focused tests, and the production client build passed.
